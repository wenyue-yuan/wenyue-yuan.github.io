About

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC). My research interests include computational mechanics, finite element analysis, damage and fracture mechanics, and phase-field modeling.

I received my Master's degree in Traffic and Transportation from Southwest Jiaotong University in 2025 and my Bachelor's degree in Transportation Equipment and Control Engineering from Central South University in 2022.

My previous research includes damage modeling of aluminum-alloy welded structures, numerical analysis of train collisions, and strength and fatigue evaluation of rail vehicle structures. My current interests also include computational approaches to damage and fracture.
